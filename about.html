OUR STORY

Mary's Tailoring Studio was founded with a simple dream — to bring the art of tailoring closer to everyone. What started as a small home studio has grown into a trusted place for custom stitching, professional alterations, and personalized fashion services.

With years of experience and a love for detail, we help clients turn their ideas into beautiful, well-fitted garments. Every stitch we make is a promise of quality, comfort, and care.
